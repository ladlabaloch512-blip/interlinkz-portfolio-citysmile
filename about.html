About CitySmile Dental Studio

A small, friendly clinic built on one simple idea: going to the dentist should feel calm, clear and fair.

Our story

Dentistry that feels calm, clear and fair

CitySmile Dental Studio was set up with one simple idea: going to the dentist should feel calm, clear and fair. We are a small team of dentists who take time with every patient, explain treatments in plain language and agree every price before work begins.

Families trust us for regular checkups, and patients who have avoided the dentist for years tell us they wish they had come sooner. Whatever your smile needs, we will help you fix it step by step, at a pace that suits you.

Our approach

Gentle dentistry, explained in plain words

We work gently, explain each step before we start, and stop whenever you need a break. There is no rushing and no pressure to decide on the spot.

Our clinic uses modern equipment, strict sterilisation for every instrument, and digital X rays with low radiation. For children, appointments are unhurried, and parents can stay with their child during treatment.

Our values

How we work, every day

Gentle first

We would rather go slowly and keep you comfortable than rush a treatment. You can ask us to stop at any point.

Clear prices

Every price is agreed before work begins and written down for larger plans. No surprises at the desk afterwards.

Honest advice

If a treatment is not right for you, we will say so, even when it means less work for us. Your teeth come first.
